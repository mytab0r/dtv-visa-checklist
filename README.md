# Thailand DTV Visa — Document Checklist (rules from 31 August 2026)

A free, source-linked checklist for the **Destination Thailand Visa (DTV)**, the 5-year
multi-entry visa for remote workers, soft-power activities and DTV dependants.

Since **31 August 2026** Thai missions accept a DTV application only from **nationals or
permanent residents of the country where you apply**, and every applicant adds a
**criminal record clearance certificate**. Applying as a tourist in Vientiane, Ho Chi Minh
City, Penang, Jakarta or Hanoi no longer works. Applications submitted *and paid* before
31 August 2026 keep the old rules.

## Required documents (DTV workcation / remote work)

| # | Document | Notes |
|---|----------|-------|
| 1 | Passport | valid 6+ months |
| 2 | Photo | taken within 6 months |
| 3 | Bank statement | last 3 months, own name, closing balance >= 500,000 THB (London lists GBP 12,000) |
| 4 | Employment contract / employer letter | dated, signed, stamped - or freelance contracts + portfolio |
| 5 | Proof of nationality or permanent residence in the country of application | passport for citizens; UK ILR, US green card, Swiss permit, German Meldebescheinigung for others |
| 6 | Criminal record certificate | from country of nationality or of application; 3 months validity in several missions, 6 months in London |

## Why files get refused

1. **Name mismatch** - statement issued to `PETROFF`, passport says `PETROV`.
2. **Statement too short** - 2 months instead of 3, or a single closing-balance printout.
3. **Balance dips** below 500,000 THB inside the statement period (gift/loan that left).
4. **Employer letter** without date, signature or stamp.
5. **Wrong mission** - applying where you are neither a national nor a permanent resident:
   refused, fee lost (~360-400 EUR, non-refundable).
6. **Criminal record certificate** missing or older than the mission allows.
