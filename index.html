Thailand DTV visa checklist 2026 — documents, criminal record, residence proof

Since 31 August 2026 Thai missions accept a Destination Thailand Visa only from nationals or permanent residents of the country where you apply, and every applicant adds a criminal record clearance certificate. Applying as a tourist in Vientiane, Ho Chi Minh City, Penang, Jakarta or Hanoi no longer works.

Documents

  1. Passport valid 6+ months; photo taken within 6 months.
  2. Bank statement for the last 3 months in your own name, closing balance from 500,000 THB (London lists 12,000 GBP).
  3. Employment contract or employer letter (dated, signed, stamped) or freelance contracts plus portfolio.
  4. Proof of nationality or permanent residence in the country of application — passport for citizens, residence permit or green card for others. Tourist stamps, hotel bookings and rental contracts do not count.
  5. Criminal record certificate from the country of nationality or of application; valid 6 months in London, 3 months in several missions.

Why files get refused

  • Name on the statement differs from the passport (PETROV vs PETROFF).
  • Balance dipped below 500,000 THB, or fewer than 3 months of statement.
  • Employer letter without date, signature or stamp.
  • Filing where you are neither national nor permanent resident — refused, fee lost.
  • Missing or outdated criminal record certificate.
